Undergraduate research

Students can work alongside graduate researchers and postdoctoral fellows on inorganic and materials chemistry. Topics listed by the group include solid-state synthesis, porous materials, chalcogenide glasses and molten-metal flux chemistry.

Contact Professor Kanatzidis to discuss a project and the expected academic-year commitment. Summer opportunities depend on available support and mutual agreement.
