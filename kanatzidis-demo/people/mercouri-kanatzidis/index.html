Biography

Exploring the chemistry of new materials.

Mercouri Kanatzidis earned his undergraduate chemistry degree at Aristotle University and his PhD at the University of Iowa in 1984. After postdoctoral research at the University of Michigan and Northwestern, he joined Michigan State University in 1987. He moved to Northwestern in 2006 and also holds an appointment at Argonne National Laboratory.

Education

Ph.D., Chemistry · University of Iowa, 1984
Research
