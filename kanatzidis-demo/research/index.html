Research

Chemistry opens new possibilities.

Explore the materials, synthesis methods and physical properties studied across the group.

01 / Chalcogenides & multianionic materials

Exploratory synthesis ↗

Discovering new compounds through molten-flux chemistry, crystal growth and systematic composition studies.

02 / Electronic transport & phonon engineering

Thermoelectrics ↗

Discovering high-performance thermoelectrics through new structures and transport mechanisms.

03 / X-rays, γ-rays & neutrons

Radiation detection ↗

Growing semiconductor crystals for sensitive radiation measurements at room temperature.

04 / Solar cells & optoelectronics

Perovskites ↗

Exploring halide perovskites and perovskitoids for light absorption, emission and energy conversion.

05

Critical minerals and environmental remediation ↗

Using layered metal sulfides to capture selected ions from water.

Nonlinear optics ↗

Developing nonlinear optical crystals with infrared transparency and strong optical responses.
