Research

Critical minerals and environmental remediation

Research focus

Using layered metal sulfides to capture selected ions from water.

Exchangeable cations in layered sulfides provide chemical selectivity for cesium, strontium and heavy metals. Synthesis and sorption studies connect framework chemistry with water remediation.

  • Open-framework solids
  • Layered metal sulfides
  • Cs⁺ & Sr²⁺ capture
  • Heavy-metal removal
