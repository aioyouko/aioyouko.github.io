Research

Nonlinear optics

Chalcogenide crystals & infrared optics

Research focus

Developing nonlinear optical crystals with infrared transparency and strong optical responses.

Chalcogenide synthesis and crystal growth are paired with optical measurements. The program includes Northwestern researchers James Rondinelli and Mercouri Kanatzidis, with Venkatraman Gopalan at Penn State.

  • Second-harmonic generation
  • Infrared transmission
  • Laser damage threshold
  • Single-crystal growth
