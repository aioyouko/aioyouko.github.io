Research

Radiation detection

X-rays, γ-rays & neutrons

Research focus

Growing semiconductor crystals for sensitive radiation measurements at room temperature.

Crystal purification, growth and device design connect materials chemistry to radiation spectroscopy. CsPbBr₃ is a major platform; lithium-containing chalcogenides support neutron detection.

  • CsPbBr₃ single crystals
  • Bridgman growth
  • Charge transport
  • Energy-resolving detectors
