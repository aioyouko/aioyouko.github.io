Laboratory resources

Tools for materials discovery.

Synthesis, crystal growth, structural characterization and physical-property measurements.

＋Synthesis and crystal-growth laboratoryImage placeholder

Synthesis & crystal growth

  • Nitrogen-atmosphere gloveboxes; Schlenk and high-vacuum lines
  • Computer-controlled high-temperature furnaces
  • Three-zone Bridgman and rocking furnaces
  • RF induction furnace; arc-welding station
  • Glovebox melt-spinning apparatus
  • Dr. Sinter-Lab SPS-515 S spark plasma sintering
  • Struers Accutom-50 precision crystal saw
＋Diffraction and thermal-analysis instrumentsImage placeholder

Structure & thermal analysis

  • STOE IPDS 2 / 2T single-crystal diffractometers
  • Rigaku MiniFlex600 powder X-ray diffractometer
  • INEL position-sensitive X-ray detector
  • Shimadzu TGA-50, DSC-50 and DTA-50
  • Micromeritics ASAP 2020 gas-sorption analyzer
＋Thermoelectric transport measurementsImage placeholder

Electrical & thermal transport

  • ULVAC ZEM-3 Seebeck / electrical-resistance system
  • Netzsch LFA-457 laser-flash diffusivity instruments
  • MMR electrical-resistivity equipment
  • Room-temperature Hall-effect system
  • High-temperature four-probe conductivity apparatus
＋Optical spectroscopy laboratoryImage placeholder

Optical characterization

  • Shimadzu UV–visible–near-IR spectrophotometer
  • Biorad-Digilab FT-Raman spectrometer
  • Nicolet 740 and 6700 FT-IR spectrometers
  • Riken-Keiki AC-2 photoemission yield spectroscopy
  • KP Technology ambient-pressure photoemission
  • Hamamatsu xenon lamp and photochemical setups

Equipment names follow the original laboratory inventory. Contact the group about current availability and access. Original equipment specifications ↗
